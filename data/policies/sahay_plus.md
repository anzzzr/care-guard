# Sahay Health: Plus Plan Policy Wording

> Sahay Health is a fictional insurer. This document is synthetic and for demonstration only.

## 1. About this plan

1.1 The Sahay Plus Plan is an individual health insurance policy with a sum insured of ₹10,00,000 (ten lakh rupees) per policy year.

1.2 The policy year starts on the policy start date shown in your policy schedule and renews every 12 months.

1.3 The sum insured is the most we will pay for all claims made in one policy year.

## 2. What is covered

2.1 In-patient hospitalisation: room, nursing, doctor fees, surgery, medicines and tests, when you are admitted on a doctor's advice for at least 24 consecutive hours.

2.2 Pre-hospitalisation expenses for 60 days before admission and post-hospitalisation expenses for 90 days after discharge, for the same illness or injury.

2.3 Day-care procedures listed in Section 8, even when the hospital stay is less than 24 hours.

2.4 Road ambulance charges up to ₹5,000 per hospitalisation.

2.5 A free annual health check-up worth up to ₹2,000 after every claim-free policy year.

2.6 Injuries caused by an accident are covered from the first day of the policy.

## 3. Room rent and ICU

3.1 There is no room rent limit under the Plus Plan. You may choose any room category, including a single private room, and ICU charges are paid at actual cost.

3.2 Because there is no room rent limit, no proportionate deduction is applied to other medical expenses.

## 4. Waiting periods

4.1 Initial waiting period: any illness that starts within the first 30 days of the policy is not covered. This waiting period does not apply to accidents.

4.2 Pre-existing diseases: any condition you had, or were diagnosed with, in the 48 months before the policy start date is covered only after 2 years of continuous coverage.

4.3 Listed procedures: the following procedures are covered only after 2 years of continuous coverage: cataract surgery, hernia repair, kidney stone removal, knee and other joint surgery (including arthroscopy and joint replacement), sinus surgery, piles and fistula surgery, gallbladder removal, and hysterectomy.

4.4 Waiting periods are counted from the first policy start date and continue across renewals as long as there is no break in coverage.

## 5. Co-payment and sub-limits

5.1 There is no co-payment under the Plus Plan at any age.

5.2 Cataract surgery is covered up to ₹50,000 per eye per policy year.

## 6. Maternity

6.1 Maternity expenses are covered after a waiting period of 3 years of continuous coverage.

6.2 The maternity benefit is up to ₹50,000 for a normal delivery and up to ₹75,000 for a caesarean delivery, for at most two deliveries during the lifetime of the policy.

6.3 A newborn baby is covered from birth for 90 days within the mother's sum insured.

## 7. Exclusions

We do not pay for:

7.1 Cosmetic or aesthetic treatment, including plastic surgery, unless it is needed to treat an injury from an accident or a burn.

7.2 Dental treatment, unless it needs hospitalisation because of an accident.

7.3 Spectacles, contact lenses and hearing aids.

7.4 Out-patient (OPD) consultations, tests and medicines when you are not hospitalised, except the annual health check-up in Section 2.5.

7.5 Treatment taken at home (domiciliary treatment), including home nursing and home physiotherapy.

7.6 Infertility and assisted reproduction treatment.

7.7 Injury that is self-inflicted or caused by alcohol or drug abuse.

7.8 Experimental or unproven treatment.

7.9 Non-medical items listed in Section 5 of the Claims Process document.

## 8. Day-care procedures

8.1 Day-care procedures are treatments that need less than 24 hours in hospital because of modern medical technology. Covered day-care procedures include cataract surgery, chemotherapy, radiotherapy, dialysis, lithotripsy (kidney stone removal), tonsillectomy, endoscopic procedures and angiography.

8.2 The waiting periods in Section 4 also apply to day-care procedures.

## 9. Network hospitals and cashless treatment

9.1 Cashless treatment is available at Sahay network hospitals. The network includes Sunrise Multispeciality Hospital (Pune), Lotus City Hospital (Nagpur), Riverbank Medical Centre (Kochi), Greenfield Hospital (Jaipur), Harbourview Heart Institute (Mumbai) and Silver Oak Hospital (Bengaluru).

9.2 At a hospital outside the network you pay the hospital first and then claim reimbursement from us. See the Claims Process document.
