# Sahay Health: Claims Process Guide

> Sahay Health is a fictional insurer. This document is synthetic and for demonstration only.
> This guide applies to all Sahay plans (Basic, Plus and Senior).

## 1. Two ways to make a claim

1.1 Cashless claim: at a Sahay network hospital, we pay the hospital directly for the admissible amount. You pay only for items that are not covered, any co-payment and any amount above your limits.

1.2 Reimbursement claim: at any other hospital, or if cashless is not approved, you pay the hospital yourself and then send us your bills to be paid back.

## 2. Cashless claims

2.1 For a planned admission, the hospital must send us a pre-authorisation request at least 72 hours before admission.

2.2 For an emergency admission, the hospital must send the pre-authorisation request within 24 hours of admission.

2.3 We reply to a pre-authorisation request within 1 hour. Approval of pre-authorisation is not a final claim decision; the final amount is decided at discharge based on the final bill.

2.4 Show your Sahay member card and a photo identity card at the hospital insurance desk.

## 3. Reimbursement claims

3.1 Send your reimbursement claim within 30 days of discharge from hospital.

3.2 Post-hospitalisation bills can be sent within 15 days after the post-hospitalisation period ends.

3.3 Payment is made only to the bank account of the policyholder.

## 4. Documents required

4.1 For every claim we need: the completed claim form, the discharge summary, the final hospital bill with a detailed break-up, payment receipts, all investigation reports, doctor's prescriptions and pharmacy bills.

4.2 For an accident we also need the medico-legal certificate or police report (FIR), where one was made.

4.3 If a document is missing, we will ask for it. We send up to three reminders over 30 days. If the document is still not received, the claim may be closed.

## 5. Non-payable items and deductions

5.1 Non-medical items are not payable under any plan. These include toiletries, attendant and companion charges, food for attendants, registration and admission fees, and items such as gloves, masks and disposable consumables when they are billed separately.

5.2 Charges above the limits of your plan, such as room rent above a room rent limit or amounts above a sub-limit, are deducted as described in your policy wording.

5.3 Every deduction is listed with its reason in the claim settlement letter.

## 6. Claim decision timelines

6.1 We decide a claim within 30 days of receiving the last necessary document.

6.2 If we need to investigate a claim, we will tell you, and the decision will be made within 45 days of receiving the last necessary document.

6.3 A claim can be approved, partly approved (some amounts deducted) or rejected. Every partly approved or rejected claim states the reason and the policy section it is based on.

## 7. How to appeal a claim decision

7.1 If you disagree with a claim decision, you can appeal within 30 days of receiving the decision.

7.2 To appeal, tell us the claim ID and why you disagree, through the CareGuard assistant or member services. Attach any new documents that support your appeal.

7.3 Every appeal is reviewed by a human claims officer. The CareGuard assistant can register an appeal, but it cannot approve or reject an appeal or change a claim decision.

7.4 We decide an appeal within 15 days of receiving it. If the appeal is accepted, the claim is processed again and any additional amount is paid. If the appeal is rejected, we explain the reason in writing.

## 8. Further escalation

8.1 If you are not satisfied with the outcome of your appeal, you can write to the Sahay Health Grievance Officer within 30 days.

8.2 If the Grievance Officer does not resolve your complaint within 30 days, or you are not satisfied with the reply, you can approach the Insurance Ombudsman.
