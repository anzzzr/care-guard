# Sahay Health: Basic Plan Policy Wording

> Sahay Health is a fictional insurer. This document is synthetic and for demonstration only.

## 1. About this plan

1.1 The Sahay Basic Plan is an individual health insurance policy with a sum insured of ₹3,00,000 (three lakh rupees) per policy year.

1.2 The policy year starts on the policy start date shown in your policy schedule and renews every 12 months.

1.3 The sum insured is the most we will pay for all claims made in one policy year.

## 2. What is covered

2.1 In-patient hospitalisation: room, nursing, doctor fees, surgery, medicines and tests, when you are admitted on a doctor's advice for at least 24 consecutive hours.

2.2 Pre-hospitalisation expenses for 30 days before admission and post-hospitalisation expenses for 60 days after discharge, for the same illness or injury.

2.3 Day-care procedures listed in Section 8, even when the hospital stay is less than 24 hours.

2.4 Road ambulance charges up to ₹2,000 per hospitalisation.

2.5 Injuries caused by an accident are covered from the first day of the policy.

## 3. Room rent and ICU limits

3.1 Room rent is covered up to 1% of the sum insured per day, which is ₹3,000 per day. ICU charges are covered up to 2% of the sum insured per day, which is ₹6,000 per day.

3.2 If you stay in a room that costs more than the room rent limit, we pay the associated medical expenses (such as doctor fees, nursing and surgery charges) in the same proportion as the room rent limit bears to the actual room rent. Medicines, implants and diagnostic tests are not reduced. For example, if you choose a room costing ₹6,000 per day, we pay ₹3,000 per day of room rent and 50% of the associated medical expenses.

## 4. Waiting periods

4.1 Initial waiting period: any illness that starts within the first 30 days of the policy is not covered. This waiting period does not apply to accidents.

4.2 Pre-existing diseases: any condition you had, or were diagnosed with, in the 48 months before the policy start date is covered only after 3 years of continuous coverage.

4.3 Specific procedures: the following procedures are covered only after 2 years of continuous coverage: cataract surgery, hernia repair, kidney stone removal, knee or hip joint replacement, sinus surgery, piles and fistula surgery, gallbladder removal, and hysterectomy.

4.4 Waiting periods are counted from the first policy start date and continue across renewals as long as there is no break in coverage.

## 5. Co-payment and sub-limits

5.1 Co-payment: if the insured member is aged 60 years or above on the date of admission, the member pays 20% of every admissible claim amount and we pay the remaining 80%. Members below 60 years of age have no co-payment.

5.2 Cataract surgery is covered up to ₹25,000 per eye per policy year.

5.3 Co-payment is applied after all other limits and deductions.

## 6. Maternity

6.1 Maternity, childbirth and newborn baby expenses are not covered under the Basic Plan.

## 7. Exclusions

We do not pay for:

7.1 Cosmetic or aesthetic treatment, including plastic surgery, unless it is needed to treat an injury from an accident or a burn.

7.2 Dental treatment, unless it needs hospitalisation because of an accident.

7.3 Spectacles, contact lenses and hearing aids.

7.4 Out-patient (OPD) consultations, tests and medicines when you are not hospitalised.

7.5 Treatment taken at home (domiciliary treatment), including home nursing and home physiotherapy.

7.6 Infertility and assisted reproduction treatment.

7.7 Injury that is self-inflicted or caused by alcohol or drug abuse.

7.8 Experimental or unproven treatment.

7.9 Non-medical items listed in Section 5 of the Claims Process document.

## 8. Day-care procedures

8.1 Day-care procedures are treatments that need less than 24 hours in hospital because of modern medical technology. Covered day-care procedures include cataract surgery, chemotherapy, radiotherapy, dialysis, lithotripsy (kidney stone removal), tonsillectomy and endoscopic procedures.

8.2 The waiting periods in Section 4 also apply to day-care procedures.

## 9. Network hospitals and cashless treatment

9.1 Cashless treatment is available at Sahay network hospitals. The network includes Sunrise Multispeciality Hospital (Pune), Lotus City Hospital (Nagpur), Riverbank Medical Centre (Kochi) and Greenfield Hospital (Jaipur).

9.2 At a hospital outside the network you pay the hospital first and then claim reimbursement from us. See the Claims Process document.
